Item Collector: When looking at a vault or fridge, use the following commands:

Name Command Information
Oil Collector/collectoilForces the selected Storage Box or Vault to collect oil from Tusoteuthis and Basilosaurus in the area.
Phoenix Pearl Collector/collectpearlsForces the selected Storage Box or Vault to collect Phoenix pearls in the area.
Achatina Paste Collector/collectpasteForces the selected Storage Box or Vault to collect Achatina Paste in the area.
Achatina Organic Polymer Collector/collectpolyForces the selected Storage Box or Vault to collect Achatina Organic Polymer in the area and convert it to regular polymer.
Crystal Collector/collectcrystalsForces the selected Storage Box or Vault to collect Gacha crystals in the area.
Fertilized Egg Collector/collecteggsfertForces the selected refrigerator or preserving bin to collect fertilized eggs in the area.
Egg Collector/collecteggsForces the selected refrigerator or preserving bin to collect dropped eggs in the area.
Poop Collector/collectpoopForces the selected Storage Box or Vault to collect poop in the area.
Pellet Collector/collectpelletsForces the selected Storage Box or Vault to collect owl pellets in the area.

Virtual Vaults: Commands for storing and retrieving items to your virtual vault (Vaultorama).

Command Description
/upload vaultnameUploads the contents of the container you are looking at to the specified virtual vault. Replace vaultname with your desired name.
/download vaultnameDownloads the contents of the specified virtual vault into the container you are looking at. Replace vaultname with your desired name.

Join an ARK Survival Evolved Server

One-click join (Steam / PC): make sure Steam is running, then click Join via Steam on a map. Steam will launch ARK and connect you to that map.
  1. If it doesn't connect, click the address on the map card to copy it.
  2. In ARK, press Tab to open the console, type open followed by a space, paste the address, and press Enter.

Join an ARK Ascended Server

ARK Ascended doesn't support Steam join links, so use either method below:
  1. Server list: Join Game → filter Unofficial → search TechyServers → Join.
  2. Direct connect (PC): in Settings → Advanced, turn on Console Access. Click Copy join command on a map below, press Tab or ~ on the main menu, paste, and hit Enter.
